**Document Version:** 2.0.0 · **Date:** November 2026 · **Author:** Vijay Paliwal (Solution Architect & Lead Developer)

## 1. Upcoming Production Integrations

1. **CRM Two-Way Sync:**
   - Salesforce & HubSpot bidirectional contact syncing.
   - Automatic opportunity creation when reply intent is classified as `interested`.
2. **Multi-Channel Sequence Extension:**
   - LinkedIn connection request & InMail automation triggers via FastMCP tools.
   - SMS / WhatsApp notification integration via WapiDesk gateway.
3. **Automated Calendar Booking Injection:**
   - Cal.com & Calendly real-time availability lookup inside generated email responses.
4. **AI Voice Follow-up (AuroraDesk Bridge):**
   - Autonomous conversational voice follow-up for high-intent prospects requesting instant callback.
